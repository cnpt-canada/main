practices

Three practices. A project can use one of them or all three.

What cnpt does.

Information system design for organisations. We audit existing systems and data structures, redesign how information moves through the business, and identify where AI can be applied. Delivered as system maps, data models, and an implementation roadmap.

System mapsData modelsAI adoptionInternal tools

Human-centred design for consumer services. We run user research, map the customer journey, and design the interface and service flow. Delivered as research findings, journey maps, and a working UI system.

ResearchJourney mapsService flowsUI systems

Design fabrication for physical products and spaces. We take an approved design into production — material selection, detail drawings, tolerances, and coordination with suppliers through to finished output.

Detail designMaterialsFabricationProduction handover

All three practices share one team and one method, so a project can move between them without changing vendors.

process

Four stages. Each one ends with something you can act on without us.

You can stop after any stage and still own something useful.

1

Frame

We interview your team and your customers, audit what exists, and rewrite the brief. Half of our engagements change shape at this point.

2–3 weeks
2

Concept

Two or three genuinely different directions, built far enough to test. We recommend one and say plainly what it costs you.

3–5 weeks
3

System

The chosen direction becomes a system — data model, components, spatial standards, and the rules that keep them consistent.

6–12 weeks
4

Entry

We design the launch sequence, run it with your team, and stay through the first weeks of real traffic to fix what only real use reveals.

4–8 weeks
what you get

Working material, handed over with the rules attached.

Every engagement ends with assets your team can operate. We write the rules down because a system nobody can maintain is a system that lasts one quarter.

Concept dossierPositioning, the experience model, and what we decided against
System mapData structures and operating flow, drawn to be maintained
Design systemComponents, tokens, and the written rules behind them
Entry planThe launch sequence, with owners and dates
HandoverTwo working sessions with your team, then thirty days on call
fit

Worth reading before you write to us.

We are a good fit for some projects and a bad fit for others.

Write to us when

  • You are entering a new market, category, or country
  • The concept still needs to be decided, not just decorated
  • Digital and physical touchpoints both matter to the outcome
  • You want a system your own team will run afterwards
  • You would rather hear the real answer than the comfortable one

Look elsewhere when

  • The direction is already locked and you need execution volume
  • You need a deck to justify a decision that has been made
  • The timeline is under four weeks end to end
  • Research findings are not allowed to change the brief
