process

For our biggest projects

You can stop after any stage and still own something useful.

  1. Understand

    We talk to your team and customers, review what you already have, and rewrite the project goals if needed. Directions can change on a whim and affect the rest of the process, so we make sure to nail it down here.

    2–3 weeks

  2. Conceptualize

    We create several ideas and narrow them down to two or three finalists before building them for testing. We lay out our findings, recommendations, and tell you plainly what it will cost.

    1-2 weeks

  3. Build

    We turn the chosen idea into a complete design based on feedback from prior stages. We make sure every screen, space, and piece fits into a system with clear rules so everything stays consistent

    2-4 weeks

  4. Deploy

    We plan your launch, run it with your team, and remain on standby for the first few weeks to fix any bugs or issues that were only revealed through public, real-world usage

    3 weeks
